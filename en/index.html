What you get

End-to-end encryption

File contents and the names of files and folders are encrypted on your device. The server gets encrypted data only and cannot read it.

Sync folder

Pick one folder on your computer. Whatever you put in it appears in TOG Drive and on your other computers, and the other way round.

Sharing with other accounts

Share a folder read-only or with edit rights. A key fingerprint check confirms you are sharing with the right person.

Public links

Links with an optional password, expiry and download limit. The key is part of the link and never reaches the server.

Versions and trash

Download or restore older versions of a file. Deleted items stay in the trash for 30 days.

Recovery phrase

24 words only you have. If you forget your password, the phrase gets you back in. Nobody else can do that for you.

Several devices

Sign in on each of your computers. Revoke a lost or stolen device from another one and it can no longer open the account.

Storage on the TOG network

Encrypted data is kept with redundancy across independent storage providers, so losing one does not lose your files.

Getting started

  1. Download the app for Windows, macOS or Linux from the releases page.
  2. Create an account: username, password and invite code. The app creates your keys and a 24-word recovery phrase on your device.
  3. Write the phrase down on paper and keep it safe. Without your password and phrase nobody can restore your files, not even us.
  4. Upload files or turn on the sync folder.

Every step is explained in the guide. To see what happens to a file after it leaves your device, read how TOG Drive works.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics in the app.
  • We do not sell or rent data.
  • No e-mail or phone number needed for an account.
  • We cannot read your files or their names, so we cannot hand them to anyone either.

What the server does have to see is listed in the privacy policy.
