Privacy policy

Applies to the TOG Drive app (Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android) and the related service. Last updated: 18 November 2026. The Serbian version is here; if the two versions differ, the Serbian version prevails.

1. Who is the controller

The controller of personal data is Vahid Zekić, Zmaj Jovina 38, 36300 Novi Pazar, Serbia, vahidzekic@gmail.com (“we”), the operator of the TOG Drive service.

An EU representative (Art. 27 GDPR) and a data protection officer have not been appointed yet. For any question about your data, contact us directly at vahidzekic@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • Your files and their names are encrypted on your device. We cannot read them.
  • An account needs only a username and a password, and we never receive the password. We do not ask for an e-mail address or phone number.
  • The server must still see some technical data to work: rounded sizes, times, structure without names, IP addresses.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no profiling. We do not sell or rent data.

3. Data we process

3.1 Account data

  • Username you choose. Other users see it when sharing folders with you.
  • Login record (OPAQUE). The password cannot be learned from it.
  • Locked keys: the account master key locked by your password, your recovery phrase and your device keys. We cannot open it.
  • Public keys and key fingerprint, seen by users you share with.
  • Device list: a random device identifier made by the app, the device name you enter and the date added, plus revoked devices.
  • Plan, quota and storage used.
  • Invite code used to open the account, if one was needed.

3.2 Your files (encrypted)

  • Encrypted contents of files and their versions, in blocks.
  • Encrypted names of files and folders, encrypted keys and signed version records.

We cannot read this data. We still treat it as personal data and protect it under this policy.

3.3 Metadata the server necessarily sees

  • the number of files and folders and how they nest (without names);
  • sizes of encrypted blocks, rounded up (the exact file size is encrypted);
  • times of uploads, changes, deletions and sign-ins;
  • which accounts you share which folder with, and with which rights;
  • for public links: that a link exists, for which item, its expiry, limit and download count, and whether it has a password (not the password or the link key).

3.4 Technical data and logs

  • IP address and the time, type and outcome of requests, in server logs;
  • session token showing you are signed in;
  • counters for request and sign-up rate limits (per account and per IP address), to prevent abuse.

3.5 Sign-up check

When sign-up is open without an invite code, the app opens a Cloudflare Turnstile check (on a computer in your browser, on a phone in a browser view inside the app) to confirm the account is not being created by an automated program. Cloudflare, Inc. processes that check under its own privacy policy.

3.6 Support

If you write to us, we process your e-mail address and message to reply.

3.7 Data that stays on your device

The app keeps device keys (in the system keystore when available), the session token, sync settings and the URLs of public links you made on your device. These do not leave the device except as the encrypted data described above.

4. What we do not collect

  • your password or recovery phrase;
  • keys to decrypt your files, names or public links;
  • readable file contents or names;
  • e-mail, phone number, real name, postal address or payment data (there are no payments in the alpha);
  • location, contacts, photos or other device data you do not upload yourself;
  • advertising identifiers or tracking data; we use no analytics or ad SDKs.

6. Recipients and processors

  • Infrastructure provider running the Drive gateway: Contabo GmbH, Germany, as a processor under contract.
  • Storage providers on The Only Grid: independent operators holding encrypted segments. Segments contain encrypted blocks of many users and no usernames or other data showing whose they are. Providers cannot read them.
  • Cloudflare, Inc., for the sign-up check (3.5), when used.
  • Apple and Google, when you get the app from their stores, under their own terms.
  • Other users see what you show them: your username, public keys and fingerprint, and folders you share.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it and on a valid request. We can only hand over what we have, and we do not have your file contents in readable form.

We do not sell, rent or share data for advertising.

7. Where data is kept

The Drive gateway runs in Germany (EU). Storage providers on the TOG network may be in various countries, including outside Serbia and the European Economic Area; they receive encrypted segments only. For EU users the service may restrict storage to providers in the EU. Transfers from Serbia to Germany are permitted, as EU member states are considered to provide an adequate level of protection. Only encrypted segments, which the recipient cannot read, leave the EU.

8. Retention

  • Account and files: as long as the account exists.
  • Trash: 30 days after deletion, then purged.
  • Older file versions: 30 days (or longer if your plan provides it).
  • Server logs with IP addresses: 14 days, unless needed longer for a specific security incident or legal obligation.
  • Inactive free accounts: an account with no sign-in for 12 months may be deleted after a prior notice in the app.
  • Support correspondence: 12 months after the last message.
  • After deletion (of a file or an account): keys and records are deleted from the gateway at once, making the data unreadable. Encrypted bytes may remain with storage providers until the current storage deal ends, at most about 30 days, and are not renewed. Gateway database backups are deleted within 30 days.

9. Public links and abuse reports

Whoever has a public link has the key to its contents. If a public link is reported to us for illegal content, we may open that link, review it, disable it and, in serious cases, suspend the account. We cannot open private files that are not shared by link. Send reports to vahidzekic@gmail.com.

10. Security

End-to-end encryption is the basis of the service: files and names are encrypted on the device with well-reviewed algorithms, every version is signed, and sign-in does not reveal your password to the server. Connections to the server are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to servers is restricted. Because only you hold the keys, we cannot restore your password or recovery phrase if you lose them.

11. Account deletion

You can delete your TOG Drive account and all data with it at any time, free of charge.

How to request deletion

  1. If you want to keep your files, download them first; they cannot be restored after deletion.
  2. In the app: Account → Delete account (“Nalog → Obriši nalog”), confirmed with your username and password. The account is deleted at once.
  3. Or send a request to vahidzekic@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion” and your username. To confirm the account is yours, we will ask you to do a short action in the app that only the account owner can do (for example, create a folder with a name we send you). Never send us your password or phrase.

What is deleted, and when

  • Immediately on confirmation (at the latest within 7 days when you ask by e-mail): username, login record, locked keys, device list, all sessions, shares, public links and encrypted file records. From then on your data cannot be decrypted, by us or anyone else.
  • Within about 30 days: encrypted bytes held by storage providers, when the current storage deal ends and is not renewed for them.
  • Within 30 days: database backups and server logs.
  • What remains: data we must keep by law (for example a record of an abuse report), only as long as the law requires. Folders others shared with you remain theirs; what others downloaded from your shares stays with them.

The username of a deleted account cannot be taken by anyone for 30 days; after that it may become available to others.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection (PDPL) you have the right to:

  • access the data we process about you and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • erasure (see Account deletion);
  • restriction of processing;
  • data portability: you can download your files from the app at any time;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Serbia the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (www.poverenik.rs); in the EU, the authority where you live or work.

Send requests to vahidzekic@gmail.com. We answer without undue delay and within 30 days at the latest. Because we cannot read your files, the copy we provide covers account data and metadata; you download file contents yourself.

13. Children

TOG Drive is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we learn that a child under 16 opened an account without parental consent, we will delete it. Parents or guardians can contact us at the address in Contact.

14. Test version

While TOG Drive is an alpha on a test network, all data may be erased when the test network is restarted. We will announce this in the app or on this site in advance whenever possible.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. We will tell you about material changes in the app before they take effect.
